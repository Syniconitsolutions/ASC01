About ASC Consult & Construct

An engineering-driven EPC company delivering structural design, turnkey PEB and RCC construction as one responsibility.

Sheet 01 — Company Introduction

Engineering beyond drawings.

ASC Consult & Construct is an engineering-driven EPC company — not a conventional contractor. We bring structural engineering, design, procurement, fabrication, construction, testing and handover together under one roof, led by licensed and chartered structural engineer Er. Anil Shivaji Chandar.

Where a conventional contractor starts with a rate and a schedule, we start with the structure itself: the loads it must carry, the systems that make it efficient to build, and the documentation that makes it safe to operate for decades. Construction follows from that engineering, not the other way round.
